Essential Visitor Information

  • Service Stations: While card payments are accepted in town, carry cash for remote service stations.
  • Currency: Both Namibian Dollars (NAD) and South African Rand (ZAR) are accepted everywhere at 1:1 value.
  • Trading Hours: Most town shops close after 13:00 on Saturdays and remain closed on Sundays.
  • Road Conditions: Watch out for drifting sand dunes across the roadway during the final 50km into Lüderitz.
