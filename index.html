What Every Visitor Should Know

  • Service Stations: Some accept cards, but carry cash just in case.
  • Shops: Lüderitz shops close early (after 13:00) on Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays.
  • Desert Driving: Slow down for sand dunes blowing across the road on the last 50 km into town!
