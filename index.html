What To Do In Lüderitz

KOLMANSKOP

The old ghost town is buzzing with activity. Daily guided tours at 09H30 and 11H00 Mon - Sat.

DIAZ POINT

A drive around the peninsula will bring you to Diaz Point with the Diaz Cross, Light House and Coffee Shop.

BOGENFELS ARCH

Visit the Bogenfels Arch in the heart of the forbidden territory (Sperrgebiet).

What Every Visitor Should Know

  • Service Stations: Some accept cards, but carry cash just to be prepared.
  • Currency: Namibian Dollar and South African Rand are accepted at equal value.
  • Shops: Lüderitz is a small town and most shops close after 13:00 on Saturdays, Sundays, and Public Holidays.
  • Driving: Slow down when approaching sand dunes across the road in the last 50 km to prevent vehicle damage!
